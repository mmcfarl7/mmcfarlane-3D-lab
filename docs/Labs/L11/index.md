# A11 – Snap fit SLA Resin printing

## Design and Model

This step was the longest, coming up with some form of snap fit that can be used for resin. I brainstormed for a few minutes and eventually got a good idea from watching my little brother play video games. He was fighting a robot character, and I glanced over at our old action figures on the shelf, so I decided to combine the two and create a ball bearing snap fit. It would take 6 pieces, 2 legs, 2 arms, a head, and a body.
