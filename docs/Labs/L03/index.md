# L3 Designing something small

## Design
My initial brainstorm was to make something as simple as possible since my last print was too detailed and thus caused a rather lengthy printing time. I settled on a rectangular print of a little desktop knickknack that I figured my other sister would enjoy since it made me think of her. The design is simple, goofy, and it didn't require too much time to design, being just a simple 1.5 in by 1.5 in square with a .35 in extrusion with half the height being a depth extrusion for the face and eyes and tongue of the smiley face.
