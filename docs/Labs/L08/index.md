## Intro Paragraph - Smart Questions
Using the website https://www.toptal.com/designers/product-design/design-thinking-great-questions we were tasked with writing a paragraph about "Smart Questions" and why they matter. To me, the answer is more about micro or macro management to lead a design. Macro questions are Smart questions that are simple but open ended, leading you to think critically about the results and properties of a design that can lead to such a simple question being incredibly complicated like "Why did we pick this?" or "What will happen if...?" and so on and so forth. Micro questions are the refining Smart questions that polish the design like you're polishing a gemstone. If a flaw is discovered in this stage, it can reveal if the gemstone, or in this case a design, is effective, correct, or impure. These forms of Smart questions can help prevent unnecessary setbacks due to fear of asking "wrong questions" and protect the thought of asking questions as interruptions towards progress.
