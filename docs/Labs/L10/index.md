# L10 - RC 2 Sliding Fit and Resin Printing


## Research

According to page 653-654 of the Machine Handbook, the clearance fit should ensure the shaft fits snugly in the hole, but the hole should have a larger clearance than the shaft to avoid too much tightness. On page 654 I have found a table that states that if I designed a hexagonal shaft at a diameter of 0.17 inches, then it would fall under the clearance 0.12 to 0.24 Nominal Inch Ranges and have a clearance between 0.15 and 0.65, which I met but somehow during the printing process lost 0.05 inches. 

My design was intended to a be a gift for a coworker, a little standee of something fitting for her ranch: An egg with a face on it. Since she liked cute thing's I decided to make it as small and adorable as possible.

## Printing

Using Resin printers called a Form 4 and a Grey V5 resin material, I finished printing my model in about an hour and 22 minutes and then scraped off the supports and washed/cured the print in another 20 minutes. Thankfully there were no safety issues or any mistakes, but I do think my print did suffer some deformations on my part from clipping and scratching with my nails when attempting to remove the little stubs left behind by the supports. Overall, the finished product was adorable and despite it not standing due to the added weight by the stubs, it is still an adorable ornament.
